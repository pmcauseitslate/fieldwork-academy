FICTIONAL SUPPORT HANDBOOK
These excerpts and identifiers are sample evidence for the knowledge-assistant project.

[S1] Standard support response, revision 1
Status: archived. Replaced by S2 effective June 1, 2025.
Standard-tier requests receive a first response within two calendar days after submission.

[S2] Standard support response, revision 2
Status: approved, effective June 1, 2025. Supersedes S1.
A standard-tier request receives a first response within three business days after submission. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays. This is a first-response target. No resolution deadline is specified. This policy does not cover premium-tier requests.

[S3] Export retention
Status: approved, effective June 1, 2025.
A trial workspace's downloadable export expires 14 days after creation. A paid workspace's downloadable export expires 30 days after creation. Regenerating an export creates a new file with a new creation time. This document does not specify whether the underlying workspace records are deleted.

[S4] Duplicate billing review
Status: approved, effective June 1, 2025.
For a suspected duplicate charge, record the ticket number and the two transaction references and route the case to the billing review queue. Do not include payment card numbers. A billing reviewer must approve a refund before any payment action. No processing time or refund amount is promised here.

[S5] Workspace onboarding
Status: approved, effective June 1, 2025.
A workspace administrator can invite a teammate from the Members page. An invitation expires 48 hours after it is issued. If an invitation has expired, the administrator can send a new one. Only administrators may issue invitations. The procedure for a lost administrator account is outside this handbook.
